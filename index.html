What Prism does

When an insurer asks why an item is on your estimate, the answer is spread across reports, receipts, and photos. Prism reads every file in a job and connects them, and each connection links back to its source page.

  1. 01

    Consolidate information

    All your jobs in one place. Upload a job’s estimates, reports, receipts, and photos, and Prism shows how every file connects.

  2. 02

    Easy to format

    Your files stay in your format. Prism reads PDFs, including scanned pages, and JPEG or PNG photos, and makes their contents searchable.

  3. 03

    No-friction integration

    Upload the files your team already produces. You don’t have to change how you create job documents or re-enter anything.
