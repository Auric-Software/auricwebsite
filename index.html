What we’re studying

The information is there. The context is scattered.

An insurance restoration job can move through several estimates, scope changes, supporting documents, and back-and-forth with an insurer. The full story rarely lives in one place.

We’re exploring whether software can help a team follow that story without replacing the systems they already depend on.

The question on our whiteboard

Could someone understand the history of a job without opening six places and asking three people?

A few things we’re trying to understand

  1. Scope changes

    How a change moves from the original estimate to revision and approval.

  2. Decision trail

    Where teams look when they need the document behind a decision.

  3. Insurer prep

    What it takes to prepare for a conversation when the job has changed.

How we work

We don’t want to guess from a whiteboard.

We’d rather watch the work, ask plain questions, and make small things people can react to. Some ideas will be wrong. Finding that out early is part of the job.
